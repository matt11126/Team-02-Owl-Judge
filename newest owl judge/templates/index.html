Common Questions (FAQ)

{# Updated Heading #}
{# Example FAQs adjusted for C-Day context #}
Industry professionals and external faculty are typically invited by KSU's College of Computing and Software Engineering (CCSE) to judge the Computing Showcase (C-Day). If you received an invitation, please follow the registration instructions provided. If you are interested in judging future events but haven't been invited, please contact the CCSE main office. KSU Faculty usually have accounts created or follow internal sign-up procedures. {# Updated Answer #}
Students enrolled in Capstone courses or participating in specific C-Day tracks (like research or graduate presentations) will receive project submission instructions from their professors or the CCSE event organizers. This typically involves logging into OwlJudge, navigating to a 'My Projects' or 'Submit Project' area, and entering details like project title, abstract, team members, and relevant links before the official deadline. {# Updated Answer #}
The specific scoring criteria for C-Day are established by the CCSE faculty and event organizers. They may include aspects like technical achievement, innovation, presentation quality, completeness, and documentation, potentially varying slightly by showcase track. Approved judges logged into OwlJudge will see the detailed scoring rubric applicable to their assigned projects during the evaluation period. {# Updated Answer #}
Yes, judges using OwlJudge can typically modify their submitted scores and comments for any project they are evaluating up until the official judging period for C-Day closes (the closing time will be announced by organizers). Simply navigate back to the project within the platform, make your adjustments, and ensure you save the changes. {# Updated Answer #}
The visibility of a live leaderboard during C-Day is an optional feature controlled by the CCSE administrators managing the event via OwlJudge. They may choose to enable it publicly, only for logged-in participants, or keep it hidden until the final awards are announced. Please check the 'Audience View' or look for a 'Leaderboard' link on the platform during the event, or inquire with event staff. {# Updated Answer #}
