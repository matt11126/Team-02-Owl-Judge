Frequently Asked Questions

{# Example FAQs - Replace with real questions and answers #}
If you are external faculty or an industry professional invited to judge, please use the "Register Now" link on the homepage. After creating your account and logging in, navigate to your Profile page where you may find a "Request Judge Role" button (availability depends on admin configuration). An administrator will then review and approve your request. KSU faculty might be pre-registered or follow a specific internal process – please check with the event coordinator.
Project submission processes may vary by course or event. Typically, students with registered accounts will have access to a 'Submit Project' or 'My Projects' section after logging in, before the submission deadline set by the administrators. This section will guide them through providing project details like name, description, team members, and potentially uploading supporting documents or links. Please refer to specific instructions provided by your instructor or event organizer.
The specific scoring criteria (e.g., Innovation, Technical Execution, Presentation Quality, Documentation Completeness) and their point values are determined by the event administrators for each showcase. Once you are logged in as an approved judge and the judging period is active, the detailed criteria and scoring scale will be clearly presented on the voting page for each project you are assigned to evaluate.
Yes, typically judges can edit their submitted scores for any project they have evaluated up until the official judging period deadline, as set by the administrator. To do this, simply navigate back to the specific project on the voting or judging page, modify your scores or comments in the provided fields, and click the "Update Vote" or "Save Changes" button.
The visibility of the live leaderboard is controlled by the event administrators. They can choose to enable or disable it at any time during the event. It might be visible to the public, only to logged-in users, or only to judges and admins, depending on the event's requirements. Check the 'Audience View' or 'Leaderboard' link (if available) to see its current status.
