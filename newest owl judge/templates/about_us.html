Our Mission for C-Day

C-Day is the culmination of countless hours of innovation, dedication, and hard work by KSU's CCSE students. OwlJudge was developed to support this vital event by providing a modern, fair, and efficient platform for evaluating these outstanding capstone projects.

Our goals are to:

  • Ensure a consistent and transparent judging experience for all participants.
  • Streamline the scoring process for judges, saving valuable time.
  • Accurately capture and calculate results to reliably recognize student achievement.
  • Enhance engagement for the entire KSU CCSE community during C-Day.
